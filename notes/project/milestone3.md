# Project Milestone 3 (PM3)

Milestone 3 will comprise about 15% of your overall course grade.

## Goal

In the third project milestone, there are four goals:

1. First, apply the feedback you received on your milestone 2 - in particular, refine the research questions, task analysis, EDA, and your sketches as Pull Requests.

2. Then, implement the final visualizations (and the interactive elements) you planned in Milestone 2 to answer your research questions, and justify the visualization choices you made.

3. Finally, create a *new* report that includes your final visualizations and the commentary. Recall that your report may be a Markdown file or (if you're aiming for an A+), a publicly available website.

4. Confirm your contracted grade (this is your last opportunity to change/adjust your contracted grade).

## 1. Apply the feedback

Each team member should apply the feedback they receive as a separate **[Pull Request](https://docs.github.com/en/pull-requests/collaborating-with-pull-requests/getting-started/helping-others-review-your-changes)** so it is easy for the TAs to look at the changes you made.

## 2. Implement the final visualizations

You should implement the final visualizations as per the requirements of the Contracted Grade your team selected.

This is not a comprehensive list, but remember to:
- Clearly label each visualization
- Use consistent encodings across views.
- Layouts should use screen space wisely. Layouts that are too sparse force the user to scroll unnecessarily. Layouts that are too dense are cluttered.
- Interfaces should include enough headings, legends, axis labels, and any other explanatory text needed to make it comprehensible.
- Demonstrate appropriate and effective use of basic visual encodings: spatial layout, color, size, shape, etc.
- Provide a fair and unbiased representation of the underlying data.
- Have fully functioning and running Jupyter notebooks.
- Make sure your views include only the necessary data, and visualizations.
- Adhere to Python style rules.
- Easy to navigate and interpret (we shouldn't have to search for which task a viz addresses).
- Have Jupyter notebooks that run.

## 3. Create a new report

In your new report, you may assume that the reader has already read through your initial report (from Project Milestone 2) - think of it as an addendum (addition) to your first report.
Include your research questions again and for each visualization/view (i.e., which will comprise of multiple plots):
- Include a screenshot or [a gif of your visualization](https://www.techsmith.com/blog/how-to-make-a-gif/) of the view in your report (or embed the interactive plot in your website for A+ projects)
- Summarize the tasks it was designed to address and how/whether the visualizations you chose accomplish (or not accomplish) that task.
- Explain and justify the visualization choices from the theoretical principles you have been exposed to
  - Marks
  - Channels
  - Characteristics of Channels that were exploited
  - Describe the interaction
  - Characteristics of Interaction and interactivity
  - Critique the view

## 4. Choose Contracted Grade

Carefully read through the Project Description and finalize your contracted grade on the PrairieLearn submission.
You are able to increase your target by up to two letter grades (so a `C` can go to an `A`, and a `B` project can be upgraded to an `A+`).
Your assigned TA will provide you with the feedback you need to achieve the contracted grade you want - it is then up to you to implement this in the final project submission (Project Milestone 4).
