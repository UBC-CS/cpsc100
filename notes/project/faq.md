# Frequently Asked questions about the Project

- If our visualisation exceeds the expectations for our contracted grade (e.g. meet for A+ when our contract was for A), will we get extra marks, and how much?

> The intention is for you to choose your contracted grade and work up towards achieving it. There is no benefit to you for selecting a lower bar and then working to overcome it. I understand you may want to do this to hedge your bets in case you don't achieve all the requirements for an A+, but I really do recommend you choose the contracted grade that you want, and then we will help you get to it by providing you personalized feedback to get to the requirements of that grade.

- Conversely, if our visualisation fails to meet the expectations for our contracted grade (e.g. failing to meet for A), how severe will the penalty be?

> There isn't really a predefined "penalty" - we will provide you with feedback and give you an opportunity to resubmit your work so that it meets the requirements of the grade you contracted. Not meeting the expectations after repeated attempts will result in a conversation between me and your group about adjusting the contracted grade. After that, if by the end of the course, expectations for your new/adjusted contracted grade are _still_ not met, that's when a penalty will be assigned. Assuming your group has met all the requirements for at least a C project and depending on how far below expectations your group was, a penalty no larger than one full grade level will be applied to your project grade. 

- When exactly will we be able to potentially change our contracted grade?

> You'll set your contracted grade when you submit Milestone 2, and you'll be able to change it until you submit Milestone 3.
